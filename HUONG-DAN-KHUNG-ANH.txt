KOI POND - THEM KHUNG ANH VA KEO GOC DOI KICH THUOC

1. Giai nen vao thu muc cha ...\431960, de cac file nam truc tiep trong ...\431960\3810169247\.
2. Mo lai Wallpaper Engine, chon lai hinh nen Koi Pond.
3. Tren hinh nen, bam 'Chinh sua khung anh' o goc duoi phai (neu Wallpaper Engine cho phep tuong tac chuot).
4. Bam '+ Them khung anh' de them khung moi, toi da 16 khung.
5. Keo giua khung de di chuyen, keo o vuong mau xanh tai goc duoi phai de phong to/thu nho.
6. Bam 'Chon anh' de chon anh, 'Xoa anh' de bo anh, 'Xoa khung' de xoa khung.
7. Bam 'Hoan tat chinh sua' de khoa vi tri. Vi tri, kich thuoc, anh tai len duoc luu trong localStorage cua hinh nen.
8. Neu khong bam duoc nut tren desktop, vao cai dat Wallpaper Engine bat 'Khung anh - Bat che do chinh sua'. Wallpaper Engine can cho phep tuong tac chuot voi wallpaper.
9. Trong cai dat WE co them thanh truot 'Khung anh 1/2/3 - Kich thuoc (px)' cho 3 khung dau.

Luu y: Wallpaper Engine khong ho tro tao them dong chon file tuy y trong bang cai dat theo so khung. Khung thu 4 tro di duoc them va chon anh truc tiep tren hinh nen. Chua kiem thu truc tiep trong Wallpaper Engine.
